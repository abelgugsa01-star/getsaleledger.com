What the three balances mean

Bank adjusted balance = statement ending + deposits in transit − outstanding checks. Trust control balance = opening + receipts − disbursements. Consignor subledger = the sum of what is owed to each person. If 1 ≠ 2 something hit the bank that is not in your books (or vice-versa). If 2 ≠ 3 your books are internally inconsistent — someone was over- or under-paid, or money is sitting in trust with no owner. Every difference needs a named explanation, never a plug.
