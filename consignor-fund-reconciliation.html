Consignor Fund Reconciliation for Auction Companies

For auction companies holding money owed to consignors: reconcile the records that explain what came in, what went out, and what is still owed. SaleLedger brings the three sides of the reconciliation into one local Windows workflow.

Try SaleLedger

Three records, one explanation

Compare auction transactions, adjusted trust bank activity, and the amount still owed to consignors. Bank cash alone cannot tell you which consignor owns the balance. A settlement report alone cannot prove that a payment cleared the bank.

Example: a payment recorded but not cleared

Suppose a consignor ledger records a $2,400 payment on the last day of the month, while the bank statement shows no cleared payment yet. Keep the payment reference and date, then establish whether it is an outstanding payment or a recording error. Do not create a second payment merely to make two reports look alike. This is an illustrative review scenario, not a real customer result.

Review the exception, then preserve the evidence

SaleLedger flags discrepancies for review. Compare the exception with the settlement, bank activity, and accounting export; document the supported correction or timing difference. Keep the report and supporting records together so another reviewer can follow the explanation.

Fit it into the auction office workflow

Collect supported auction and accounting exports plus the bank statement for the same period. Keep identifying references intact. Review each open item with the person responsible for settlements, then retain the reproducible reconciliation output. SaleLedger supports this review; it does not run auctions or replace your general ledger.

Questions before you start

Does a matching bank balance prove consignor balances are correct?

No. You still need to establish which amounts are owed to which consignors and reconcile those liabilities with the transaction records.

Does SaleLedger automatically resolve discrepancies?

No. It flags items for review. Your team determines the supported explanation or correction.

Can I use Auction Flex records?

The export guide describes supported Auction Flex and QuickBooks/IIF inputs. Check the required files before starting; this is an export-based workflow.
