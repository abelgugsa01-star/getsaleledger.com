How to read it

  • The status line is the answer: IN BALANCE or OUT OF BALANCE. There is no "close enough".
  • The three balances are the three-way check — bank adjusted, trust control, consignor subledger. Here the bank holds $16,255.00 more than the books and the books hold $475.00 more than consignors are owed; the exceptions below account for every dollar of both.
  • Each exception is one fact with a name, a date and an amount: an overpayment to chase, a deposit with no auction behind it, a transfer to operating with no settlement, a bank fee taken from trust. SaleLedger never resolves one for you.
  • The check register (date, payee, check number, amount) is the record Wisconsin SPS 125.12 and similar rules ask for. It is produced from the QuickBooks/IIF file, not retyped.
  • The report prints, saves as CSV, and can be emailed from the app; every run is kept in a local history.
